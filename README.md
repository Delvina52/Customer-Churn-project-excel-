# 📊 Bank Customer Churn Analysis (Excel)

### Who is leaving the bank, and who should we try to keep?

An Excel project using **pivot tables, formulas, charts and slicers** to find which customers churn the most and what to do about it.

---

## 🏦 Project Overview

**Dataset:** Kaggle – Bank Customer Churn Prediction (10,000 customers)

**Tools:** Excel (Pivot Tables, AVERAGEIFS, SUMIFS, Charts, Slicers)

**Key Analysis:**

- Total customers, churned customers & churn rate (10,000 / 2,037 / 20.4%)
- Churn by country, gender, age band, number of products, activity status & balance
- Highest-risk groups: Germany (32.4%), age 50-59 (56%), inactive members (26.9%), 1-product customers (27.7%)
- Average tenure of churned vs retained customers (4.9 vs 5.0 years, so tenure has little effect)
- Interactive dashboard with KPI cards, charts & slicers

**Business Value:**
Helps the bank identify which customers are most likely to leave, so it can target retention offers, re engage inactive members and cross sell a second product to single product customers.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Focus retention on **Germany** and customers aged **40-59**
- Re-engage **inactive members** with offers or calls
- Push **single-product** customers to take a second product
